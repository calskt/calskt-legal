CALSKT Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

CALSKT ("the app") is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with "Sign in with Apple," we receive a unique account identifier and, depending on your choice, your name and email address (or Apple's private relay email).
  • Profile information: Age, weight, height, biological sex, and activity level, which you provide to calculate your personal calorie needs.
  • Food and activity logs: The plain-text descriptions you type when logging a meal or activity, along with the calorie and macronutrient estimates generated from them.
  • Subscription status: Whether you have an active CALSKT Pro subscription, its renewal date, and related billing state (e.g. active, expired, in a billing grace period) as reported by Apple. We never receive your payment card details — Apple handles all billing directly.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any HealthKit data, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics/tracking.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to operate the app: authenticating you, calculating your calorie targets, generating nutrition estimates for the meals and activities you log, and keeping your subscription status up to date so the app knows what features you're entitled to.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple — provides sign-in and processes all subscription payments.
  • Anthropic — the text you enter when logging a meal or activity is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a calorie/macro estimate.
  • Supabase — our database provider, which stores your account, profile, logs, and subscription status on our behalf.

We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not share it with any party beyond the services listed above.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a database we control, protected by row-level security so that only your own account can read your own data.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

CALSKT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
