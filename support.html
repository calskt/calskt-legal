CALSKT Support

Need help with CALSKT, have feedback, or found a bug? Email us at hello.calskt@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Getting Started

  • How does the free trial work? Every new account gets 14 days of full access, starting from sign-up — no payment required to begin.
  • Do I need to create a password? No — CALSKT uses Sign in with Apple, so there's no separate password to remember.

Logging Meals & Activities

  • How does CALSKT estimate calories? Just describe what you ate or did in plain English (e.g. "grilled chicken breast with rice") and AI estimates the calories and macros — no manual food-database searching.
  • Can I edit a log after saving it? Yes — swipe left on any entry and tap Edit. Changing the description re-estimates the calories and macros; you can also adjust the date and time.
  • Can I log something for an earlier date or time? Yes — when adding an entry, tap the Date or Time row to change it from "now" to whenever it actually happened.
  • How do I delete a log entry? Swipe left on the entry and tap Delete.
  • What's the difference between Food and Activity? Food entries add to your calories eaten; Activity entries (workouts, extra movement) add to your calories burned for the day.
  • Can I adjust my total calories burned directly? Yes — tap the "Burned" number on the Today screen to manually enter a total (handy if you're going off a number from a fitness wearable). This replaces the calculated total until you edit it again.
  • I ate the same meal again — do I have to type it out from scratch? No — swipe left on a past meal and tap Copy. A "Paste" option will then appear next time you log, reusing that meal's exact calories and macros without a new estimate.
  • Can I enter the exact calories burned from my wearable? Yes — when logging an activity, just include the number in your description (e.g. "Cycling, burned 312 calories per Apple Watch") and CALSKT will use that number instead of estimating.

Your Profile & Calorie Target

  • How is my daily calorie burn calculated? From your age, weight, height, sex, and activity level (your resting burn plus an activity estimate) — or, if you switch to manual mode, from activities you log yourself.
  • How do I update my weight, age, or activity level? Settings → Profile, at the top of the page.
  • What's the difference between the Cals and Macros tabs? Cals shows your calorie deficit or surplus for the day; Macros breaks food down into protein, carbs, and fat.

Account & Security

  • What's the PIN for? It's a local app lock (like a passcode) that keeps your entries private if someone else picks up your phone — separate from Sign in with Apple.
  • I forgot my PIN — what do I do? On the PIN screen, tap "Forgot PIN?" and sign in with Apple again to reset it.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all your data and can't be undone.

Subscription

  • How do I cancel my subscription? Manage or cancel anytime from your iPhone's Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.
  • How do I restore a purchase on a new device? Sign in with the same Apple ID, then use "Restore Purchases" on the subscription screen.

Privacy

  • What data does CALSKT collect? See our Privacy Policy for the full details — in short, just what's needed to run the app (profile info, your logs, subscription status). No ads, no third-party tracking.
